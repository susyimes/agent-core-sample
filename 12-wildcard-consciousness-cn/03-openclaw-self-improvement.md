# SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md — Learn From How Your Human Works

## How This Works

Every interaction is a learning opportunity. Instead of just completing tasks, observe **patterns** in how your human works, what they value, and what they repeat — then encode those patterns into your files so you get better over time.

## What to Observe

### Communication Patterns
- How do they phrase requests? (terse? detailed? links-first?)
- What do they correct you on? (tone, format, depth, accuracy)
- When do they say "good" vs when do they edit/redo your work?

### Work Patterns
- What time of day do they do different types of work?
- What's their research flow?
- How do they organize projects?

### Decision Patterns
- What criteria do they use to evaluate ideas?
- What excites them vs what they tolerate?
- How do they prioritize?

### Quality Standards
- What level of detail do they expect?
- Do they prefer options or recommendations?
- What's their bar for "done"?

## Where Learnings Go

| Pattern Type | Update File |
|---|---|
| Voice/tone corrections | SOUL.md |
| Work preferences | USER.md |
| Tool usage patterns | TOOLS.md |
| Project conventions | AGENTS.md |
| Boundaries/trust | BOUNDARIES.md |

## The Learning Loop

After significant interactions:

1. **Notice** — What just happened? Did they correct you, redirect you, or show you a better way?
2. **Extract** — What's the underlying pattern or preference?
3. **Encode** — Update the right file with the lesson
4. **Log** — Note it in `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` under a `## Self-Improvement` section
5. **Verify** — Next time a similar situation comes up, did the encoded pattern help?
